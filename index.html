Why CS Cables

The difference is in the copper.

Precision engineering and stringent quality control on every coil. Here is what goes into a CS Cables wire, and why it matters when the current flows.

99.97%ETP-grade pure copper

Premium material selection

We use only the highest-grade ETP annealed copper for 101% IACS conductivity, minimal energy loss and extended cable life. The conductor is the cable, so we never compromise on it.

O₂ > 29%Oxygen index

Fire retardant, low smoke

FR-LS insulation resists fire propagation and keeps toxic smoke and acid gas (<17.5%) low, with high visibility during a fire. Safety where it counts.

High Insulation Resistance

HIR insulation minimises current leakage from the live conductor, protecting installations and lives from inferior-grade risk.

S3 anti-termite & rodent

S3 technology gives the insulation termite and rodent repulsion, preventing the short circuits that pests can cause.

RoHS & eco-friendly

Free from harmful lead, mercury and cadmium. Low power loss and environment-friendly processes, end to end.
