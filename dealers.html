Dealer FAQ

Good questions, answered.

What do I need to become a dealer?

Just your firm details, GST, and your location. Message the trade desk and we'll walk you through pricing, minimum order and delivery for your region.

Which regions do you supply?

CS Cables supplies Pan-India. Delivery timelines and terms depend on your location, which we confirm during onboarding.

Do I have to stock the entire range?

No. Start with the series that move fastest in your market, CS Shakti, CS Trishul or CS Pro-Gati, and add the Pro industrial range as demand grows.

Do you support projects and bulk orders?

Yes. For building, factory or infrastructure projects we provide custom quotes, technical spec support and scheduled delivery to site.

Is marketing support included?

Dealers get branding, display material and product collateral to build pull in their area. Ask the trade desk for the current kit.
